# Jobs

Owned by the level-designer agent. One brief per job, written **before** the builder greyboxes
it. The map contract and the job pipeline are in the moving-crew skill (`references/maps.md` and
`references/content-pipelines.md`).

Every area, job, floor plan, and bonus objective here is our own. The campaign's *shape* matches
the original (about 30 story jobs in themed areas, 3 bonus objectives each, plus arcade levels).
Its content doesn't. Never trace or recreate a level from the original.

Medal times stay `TBD` until they're measured with real runs (see GAME.md's rule).

## StarterHouse (M1–M2 greybox)

- **Fantasy:** the plainest possible move. It exists to prove the core loop.
- **Layout:** one floor, 4 rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom, hall). Front door 8 studs wide, side door 4 studs wide (a heavy item fits only when it's turned lengthwise), 2 breakable windows facing the truck, and one lever that opens a sliding garden gate (a shortcut).
- **Truck:** the small moving truck, at the curb, 20 studs from the front door; its back faces the house.
- **List:** 8 small boxes, 1 lamp (fragile), 1 vase (fragile), 1 couch (heavy), 1 armchair (heavy), 1 bed (heavy).
- **Stacking check:** everything fits in the small truck only if the bed, couch, and armchair go in first.
- **Puzzle:** the bed is in the back bedroom; the short route is through the side door, but only if it's turned.
- **Bonus objectives:**
  1. Break nothing fragile.
  2. Smash both windows.
  3. Never use the front door (the gate and the windows only).
- **Camera yaw:** 45° (from the southwest corner, looking northeast).
