## StarterHouse (M1 greybox)

- **Fantasy:** the plainest possible move. It exists to prove the core loop.
- **Layout:** one floor, 4 rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom, hall). Front door 8 studs wide, side door 4 studs wide (a heavy item fits only when it's turned lengthwise), 2 breakable windows facing the truck.
- **Truck:** at the curb, 20 studs from the front door; the truck's back faces the house.
- **List (core, crew 1–2):** 8 small boxes, 1 lamp (fragile), 1 vase (fragile), 1 couch (heavy), 1 armchair (heavy), 1 bed (heavy).
- **Extras:** `minCrew 3`: +2 boxes and 1 chair. `minCrew 5`: +3 boxes and 1 fridge.
- **Puzzle:** the bed is in the back bedroom; the short route is through the side door, but only if it's turned.
- **Camera yaw:** 45° (from the southwest corner, looking northeast).
- **Medal times:** TBD (measure in T1.8).
