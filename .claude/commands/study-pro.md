---
description: Mine everything a specific pro or coach has published and extract their playbook
argument-hint: <game> <player or channel>
---

Run `channel-miner` on $ARGUMENTS (plus `foreign-scene-scout` if they're from a non-English scene, and
`replay-analyst` if their demos/matches are public). Then write `games/<slug>/pros/<name>.md`: their signature
concepts, settings, habits, how they'd approach my main/role, and a "watch these first" list with timestamps.
