# Flick shot
**Tier:** fundamental → intermediate · **Patch checked:** Nov 2026 · **Status:** unverified (several fan guides agree)
## What it is
Pop the ball up with Flick (Q), go up with it, and shoot in the air.
## Why it wins games
Most-cited reliable way to beat the keeper: high shots to the top of the net are hard to catch.
## How to execute
1. Flick (Q) **while moving** (from a standstill is the classic mistake).
2. Jump after the ball.
3. Hold shoot until the charge meter is **full**, aim at the top of the net, release.
Variant: flick → jump → slide. The slide adds distance and hides the kick animation.
## Drill
Private server with bots: 20 flick shots from the edge of the box. Success = 12/20 on target, 6/20 goals vs bot keeper.
## Common mistakes
Standing-still flicks; partial charge; shooting at the keeper's body.
## Counterplay
A keeper who waits for the ball to leave the foot and reads the indicator.
