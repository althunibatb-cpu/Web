# Volleys and bicycle kicks
**Tier:** advanced · **Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** volley types are fan-sourced; the slide-volley removal is **confirmed** (official)

## Three volley types: choose by where the keeper is
| Input | Volley | Use |
|---|---|---|
| LMB | Kick volley | Power from range |
| RMB | Pass volley | One-touch redirect at close range or to a teammate |
| F | Lob volley | Chip a keeper who's off his line |

## Rules
- ❌ **Slide volley is dead.** You can't slide after locking onto a volley (official, Sep 8). Pre-September montages showing it are outdated. Flick, jump, slide only works if the slide comes **before** the volley lock.
- Volleys are stamina-heavy. Running dry mid-volley can drop the ball, so go in with stamina.
- Start wall volleys while already moving, not from a standstill.

## Wall lift
Play the ball off the side wall so it comes back high, then volley it over a keeper who has crouched for a low shot. Every arena has borders since the Sep update (official), so walls are available on every map.

## Self-fed bicycle kick
1. Near the opponent's goal, kick the ball into a wall so it rebounds toward you.
2. As it arrives, **jump backward** (away from the ball).
3. Fire the shot mid-air. Hold curve for a bicycle-curve.
- Timing cue from a TikTok tutorial: **wait for the spark effect** before jumping.

## Side volleys vs the AI keeper
A viewer reports that the AI keeper jumps instead of diving against side volleys (T5, test it).

## Drill
20 wall-lift volleys and 20 self-fed bicycles. Success = 50% on target, 25% scored against the in-match keeper.
