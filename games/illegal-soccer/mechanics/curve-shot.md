# Curve shots (advanced)
**Tier:** intermediate → advanced · **Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** contested input (see below)

## ⚠️ First: which button curves on your patch?
Sources disagree, and there was a "new curve" change recently:
- **Middle mouse + drag:** most fan guides.
- **Right click held while charging + mouse move:** SavePoint, describing the 14 Sep curve update. Trackpad uses T.
- **Hold shoot + hold pass + turn camera:** a mobile-first video that says it works on every device. On PC that's LMB+RMB.
- **Alt + M1 + camera turn:** mentioned in a TikTok summary.

Curve is a rebindable row in **Settings → Keybinds**, so check it there. Settings also have **Curve Sensitivity** and **Invert Curve**. Start sensitivity around 75%. Lower it only if shots bend out of play; guides range from 40% to 100%.

## Rules that are true for every input
- Spin only applies if the curve input is **held at contact.** Hold it through the charge; a late tap does nothing.
- Camera turning is slowed mid-charge (official), so pre-aim.
- Curve works on **kicks, throws and volleys** (since the ~Sep 15 curve update).

## The combos, ranked by use
| Combo | How | Difficulty | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wall-kick curve** | Kick into the side wall, curve the rebound. Two direction changes, so the keeper pivots twice. | Easy | Wide angle near the sideline |
| **Fire-ball curve** | Curve with the Fire boost active (extra ball speed) | Easy | Illegal mode only, from range |
| **Flick-up curve** | Q to pop the ball, curve on the volley at the apex | Medium | In the box vs a set keeper. Called the hardest shot to stop in Legal |
| **Bicycle curve** | Wall rebound behind you, jump backward, shoot mid-air with curve held | Hard | Wall directly behind you |

## Reading it as a keeper
A shooter dragging left means a curve right. Pre-position against Fire-ball curves rather than reacting.

## Drill
1. 10 curves each way from each wing (8/10 must bend the right way).
2. 10 wall-kick curves per side.
3. 10 flick-up curves from the penalty spot.
Then repeat all three against the in-match keeper, not just the practice-area AI, which a viewer reports is much easier.
