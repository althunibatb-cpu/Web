# Curve shot and combos
**Tier:** intermediate · **Patch checked:** Nov 2026 · **Status:** unverified
## How to execute
PC: hold middle mouse **through contact** while shooting, drag the mouse left/right to bend that way. A tap doesn't count.
Controller: hold LT+RT, push the right stick when the meter appears.
## Combos (easiest first)
- **Wall-kick curve:** kick into the side wall, curve the rebound. Good from wide angles; the keeper has to turn twice.
- **Flick-up curve:** Q, then curve at the apex/volley. Rated hardest for keepers to read (opinion).
- **Bicycle-kick curve:** needs a wall behind you and a jump-back. Hard.
## Drill
10 curved shots each way from each wing, then 10 wall-kick curves. Success = bend in the intended direction 8/10.
## Disagreements
Difficulty ratings vary between guides. One guide's "Freeze Gun + curve is the top technique" claim is uncorroborated.
