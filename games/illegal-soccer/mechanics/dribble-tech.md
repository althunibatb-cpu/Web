# Dribble and ball tech (advanced)
**Tier:** advanced · **Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** cancels **confirmed**; named techs fan-sourced (one source each)

## Dribble is a dodge, not a carry
Press Space **at the moment the defender commits** to the tackle, never in open space. It beats **one** committed defender, not a crowd. Spamming it is the most-cited mistake. A dribble costs a full stamina bar, so one wasted dribble means the next tackle wins.

## Named techs
| Tech | Input | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Flick-catch** | While moving forward, Q, rise with the ball and reclaim it before it lands | Beats a slide tackle: the slide passes under you. You did it right if the tackler goes beneath you and you keep the ball |
| **Flick-recollect redirect** | Same input, but turn in the air | Change direction mid-carry |
| **Sprint-flick carry** | Flick the ball ahead while sprinting | Faster than a normal carry, but only in open space |
| **Charge-bait** | Start a kick or pass charge, then Space out of it (official cancel) | Make the defender slide, then dodge |
| **Wall pass to self** | Rebound off the border | Cheap on stamina; works on every map |
| **Flick tackle** | Flick, jump, tackle before touching the ball again | Hard. An aerial evade plus a tackle |
| **Landmine carry** (Illegal) | Drop mines along your carry path | Chasers run into them |

## Stamina discipline
- Keep about 25%, or one full bar, in reserve for an emergency dodge.
- Reported bug: spamming jump drains stamina even when you don't leave the ground. Don't jump-spam before a flick.
- No source gives regen rates. Measure them yourself in a private server.

## Changing angles
Never carry in a straight line. Defenders pre-time their tackle against a straight carry.

## Drill
Use the "Waiting for players" lobby time: 20 flick-catches in a row, then 20 charge-baits. In a private server, have a friend slide at you 20 times. Success = keep the ball 12 or more times.
