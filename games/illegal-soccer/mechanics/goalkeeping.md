# Goalkeeping
**Tier:** fundamental · **Patch checked:** Nov 2026 · **Status:** unverified
- Step into the rectangle in front of your net to become keeper: catch, punch, dive. Sprinting out ends it.
- **Don't dive early.** Wait until the ball leaves the foot, and read the circle/trail indicator for direction.
- A shooter dragging the mouse left means a curve to the right.
- Use punch on attackers arriving at the goal mouth before they shoot.
- Dive key is disputed (F vs Q/E): check your keybinds.
- ⚠️ A "6-second immunity after a catch" is claimed by one guide and unconfirmed.
