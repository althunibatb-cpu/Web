# Goalkeeping (advanced)
**Tier:** fundamental → advanced · **Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** two official buffs **confirmed**; the rest fan-sourced

## Official changes that shape the role
- **You can't be pushed while diving** (official log). A committed dive holds its line, so attackers have to beat the dive itself.
- **Slightly longer reaction to kicks that change direction mid-charge** (official). Late fakes are weaker.
- **Less knockback** (the October and Nov 1 updates).

## The box
- Positional: stand in the rectangle and you're keeper (catch, punch, dive). Leave it and you're an outfielder.
- **If several teammates stand in the box, they all get keeper controls and none of them can shoot.** In scrambles, outfielders stay out, or they can lock the real keeper out.
- Keybinds change between patches (Dive on Q/E vs an old F-dive/G-punch layout). Trust the **HUD prompts** that appear when you become keeper.
- No published box size, catch radius or punch range. Measure them yourself.

## High-level habits
1. **Don't live on the line.** Curves beat static keepers. Shade toward the shooter's lean and set your angle before the strike.
2. **Don't dive until the ball is off the foot.** This beats the shot cancel.
3. **Punch the man, not the ball.** It's a melee knock-away on an attacker reaching the goal mouth.
4. **Distribute fast:** after a catch, outlet-pass (RMB) to a teammate. Dribbling out drops your keeper status.
5. **Barricades (up to 3 walls):** walling the near post invites a far-post shot, so shade far.
6. **Against Fire-ball curves:** pre-position. You won't react in time.
7. **Rotation:** if you push up, a teammate fills the box immediately. A keeper outside the box can score, and a volley earns the full bonus.

## How attackers beat you (so you can predict it)
- Shot fakes: wait them out.
- Rocket Punch on you right before the shot (it now hits like a full-charge kick).
- Freeze Gun or Grenade to move you, then a teammate scores.
- Flick-up and wall-kick curves.

## Unresolved
- The **6-second post-catch immunity** is repeated by fan sites but has no developer source. Some say it applies to any new carrier. Test it in a private server.
- The fan claim that keepers earn passive ranked points is contested.

## Drill
A friend shoots 30 mixed shots (curves, fakes, lobs). Success = no early dives, 15 or more saves, every catch followed by an outlet pass within 2 seconds.
