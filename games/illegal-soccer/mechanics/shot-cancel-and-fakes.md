# Shot cancel and keeper fakes
**Tier:** advanced · **Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** the cancel itself is **confirmed** (official log); the fake technique is fan-sourced

## What it is
Pressing **dribble (Space)** cancels any kick, pass, lob or flick you're charging. The official Sep 8 log says so, so it's an intended mechanic and not a bug. A charge can also end a dribble early. That gives you two uses:
1. **Shot fake:** start a power shot, cancel just before contact, and shoot the side the keeper vacated.
2. **Tackle bait:** start a charge to make a defender commit to the slide, then dribble out of it.

## How to execute (shot fake)
1. Set your aim **before** charging. Camera sensitivity is reduced mid-charge (official).
2. Hold LMB to charge. Watch for the keeper's first lean or dive.
3. Press Space **just before the ball would leave your foot.**
   - Too late and the ball fires at the keeper.
   - Too early and there's no fake animation, so the keeper doesn't bite.
4. Shoot the opposite side the moment they commit.
- **Fake-curve variant:** start the curve animation, cancel, re-aim, then fire.
- **Item swap:** equipping an item no longer cancels a held charge (official). You can carry a charge while switching to an item.

## When / when NOT
- ✅ Against **human** keepers who pre-commit.
- ❌ Against the **AI keeper.** It runs fixed logic and doesn't bite on cancels (fan wiki).
- ⚠️ It's **weaker than in old videos.** Since Sep, keepers react slightly longer to kicks that change direction mid-charge (official). Pre-September fake tutorials overstate it.

## Setup
Many competitive players bind cancel to a **side mouse button**, so they can cancel mid-charge without leaving WASD. Keep enough stamina for a cancel plus the real shot.

## Drill
Private server with a friend in goal. 30 reps: charge, cancel, shoot the opposite side. Success = the keeper dives the wrong way on 10 or more, with zero accidental shots straight at them.

## Counterplay
Keepers: don't dive until the ball is actually off the foot. Watch the ball, not the animation.
