# Fundamentals
**Patch checked:** Nov 2026 (after the Grenade/Sledgehammer update) · **Status:** unverified (fan sources)

## Controls (PC, most-cited layout)
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Shoot / power shot | Left click / hold left click |
| Pass | Right click |
| Flick | Q |
| Lob | F |
| Request ball | R |
| Dribble | Space |
| Sprint | Shift |
| Slide tackle | E |
| Curve | Hold middle mouse while shooting, drag left/right |

Controller curve: hold LT+RT and push the right stick when the meter shows.
⚠️ Guides disagree on keys (some list F = kick, Q = request, Left Ctrl = tackle). **Open Settings → Keybinds and check yours.** Remapping is PC-only.

## Settings to start with
- Assisted Passes **on**, automatic item use **off**.
- If curves don't bend, raise Curve Sensitivity.

## Stamina (fan-reported)
Dribble and slide tackle cost a full bar; kick/shot and flick about half; pass and lob about a quarter.
→ Pass to move the ball, sprint in short bursts, and arrive at every tackle with stamina left.

## Keeper zone
There's no keeper button. Stand in the rectangle in front of your own net and you become keeper (catch, punch, dive). Leave it and you lose the role. One keeper at a time.

## Five beginner rules
1. Don't shoot straight at the keeper: pass beside/behind them, or shoot to the far corner.
2. Don't ball-hog.
3. Fully charge aerial shots (half-charged ones drop into the keeper's hands).
4. Only slide when the carrier is in reach.
5. Save your best item for a moment that matters.
