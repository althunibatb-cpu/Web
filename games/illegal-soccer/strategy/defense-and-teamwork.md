# Defense and team play (4v4)
**Status:** unverified
- **Slide timing:** only when the carrier is in reach. A miss locks you in a recovery animation with nobody behind you. Press E at the closest moment; don't spam.
- **Off-ball tackles** are legal: hit the receiver before the pass arrives.
- **Shape:** one presser angled to push the carrier off the easy lane; the second covers the receiver and loose ball. Never be the third chaser.
- **After losing the ball:** recover position before diving into a tackle.
- **Stamina:** short sprint bursts; arrive with stamina left.
- **Barricades** in front of your net ragdoll attackers. Don't rely on them alone.
- **Attack:** pass beside/behind the keeper, use wall passes when low on stamina, and don't ball-hog.
