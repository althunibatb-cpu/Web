# 4v4 tactics and game sense
**Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · **Status:** Last Chance and the map changes are official; everything else comes from fan wikis.

## Defence
- **Layered:** one player blocks the shooting lane (body or barricade), one covers goal, one **holds for the rebound.** Never all collapse on the shooter.
- **Press angle:** approach from the side that pushes the carrier away from his best lane. The second defender takes the receiver and the loose ball.
- **Slides:** only in reach. A miss means a recovery animation with no one behind you. Off-ball tackles on receivers are legal.
- **Kickoff (defending):** don't sprint at the ball on the countdown. Hold near the circle, let them take the first touch, then tackle with full stamina.
- **Box discipline:** outfielders stay out of the keeper rectangle in scrambles. Several players in the box all lose shooting.
- **Go-Kart:** shoot the ball or an item into it.

## Attack
- **Crash the parry:** with no referees, shoot into the keeper to force a parry while a second runner arrives on the bounce. Always have a runner behind the shot.
- **Support spot:** the off-ball striker sits slightly ahead of or beside the carrier, outside the same challenge zone, so one defender can't cover both.
- **Refill first:** when someone leaves a role (the keeper steps out, a defender joins the attack), refill it before anyone else commits.
- **Pass beside or behind the keeper** beats shooting at them.
- **Walls everywhere:** every arena has borders, so use wall passes and wall curves on any map.

## Game state
- **Leading:** keep the ball moving with short passes rather than standing on it. Forcefield or Barricades help close it out.
- **Leading at the buzzer:** Last Chance (official) only triggers if the trailing team is down by **exactly one AND holds the ball** at time-out. **Win the ball back at the buzzer and the match ends.**
- **Trailing by one with about 30 s left:** *keep* possession through zero. A hopeful long shot that gets caught throws away the Last Chance attack.
- **Overtime:** length and golden-goal rule aren't documented. Check in-game.

## Legal vs Illegal Ranked
- **Legal (no items):** passing, dribbling, curves and keeping. The flick-up curve is the hardest shot to stop, so close down flick space early.
- **Illegal:** item ban, gadget sequencing, keep one player home against Jetpack.

## Scrims
Club roster cap is 4. Private servers can set the map, bots, match length, ball physics, jump height and items off: a full drill lab.
