# Items (advanced): uses, counters, numbers
**Patch checked:** Gamemodes (Nov 7, 2026) · Numbers marked ✅ are official; the rest are fan-sourced.
Roster: about 18 or more items now (the original 14 plus Grenade, Sledgehammer, Boomerang and Go-Kart). Live odds and the pity counter are in **Shop → Item Rolls**. Fan odds tables are outdated.

| Item | Key facts | Best use | Counter |

## Systems
- **Item ban (Illegal Ranked):** ban what the enemy actually leans on, not just the tier-list top (usually Jetpack, Rocket Punch or Freeze Gun). Sources disagree on whether it's a team vote or one ban per team.
- **Gadget sequencing:** use the first item on the entry or shot, and hold the second for the rebound or the late runner.
- **Loadouts (one fan wiki):**
  - Striker: Jetpack + Grapple
  - Presser: Rocket Punch + Freeze/Grapple
  - Keeper: Grapple + Oil
  - All-rounder: Barricades + Rocket Punch
- **Mastery:** duplicate rolls unlock up to 5 skins per item; they aren't wasted. Player Cards are cosmetic. Rolls bought with Robux buy more attempts, not better odds.
- **Settings:** keep Automatic Item Use **off** (added Aug 28).
