# Ranked
- **Queues:** Illegal (items) and Legal (no items), each with its own Elo and leaderboard. Launched around Sep 20, 2026.
- **Entry:** 5 owned items. You start with 3; the other 2 come from rolls at 1,000 Coins. Then 10 placement matches.
- **Ladder** (Sportskeeda, backed by video titles): Bronze → Silver → Gold → All-Star → Legend → World Class, each I–III, then **Hall of Fame** (top 150 only). Wikis listing Platinum, Diamond and Champion are likely wrong.
- **AFK players** are pulled from ranked matches (official ES achievements page).
- **Unknown:** seasons, resets, decay, rewards, and the real Elo weighting (one weak fan claim says wins and saves count most).
- **Common ranked mistakes** (fan wiki): silent solo-queueing, swapping roles mid-match, ball-hogging.
